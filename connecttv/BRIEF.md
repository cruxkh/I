## LOOK (all agents, strictly): the logo's world
Deep navy night (#0B1250 / #070B2E) with slow drifting colour clouds. Everything is **candy liquid + glossy gel**: paint splashes and drops (pink #FF2E93, orange #FF8A1F, yellow #FFD23F, lime #7CFF3A, cyan #19C8FF, purple #8B3DFF, blue #2F6BFF, green #19D68B), glossy white/colour gel cards with a top highlight, glossy candy titles with dark outline, neon lightning bolts (the logo's bolt), twinkling sparkles, shockwave rings, waves of liquid as scene transitions. It must feel like a premium motion-graphics spot for a streaming brand: bold, juicy, high-contrast, huge type, big shapes, a new visual idea every ~1.5 s.
ALL MOTION IS SMOOTH: springs, overshoot (`CL.pop`, `CL.spring`), easing, flowing drift. The client REJECTED stepped 12 fps stop-motion ("lag"). Never quantise time except for random seeds (`CL.qs`). The compositor renders every frame as 3 temporal samples (motion blur), so fast motion is welcome.
FORBIDDEN: paper collage / kraft / torn paper / tape / marker scribbles (that was the previous film), flat boring layouts, small timid elements, stepped animation, generic clip-art.

## HARD RULES
- Deterministic: a frame is a pure function of time t. No Math.random, no Date. Use `A.hash`, `A.rng(seed)`, `A.noise1/2`, `CL.j`.
- No em dashes or en dashes in any on-screen text. Hebrew spelled exactly as in `lines.js` (never change spelling). Hebrew text: `ctx.direction='rtl'`; Latin: 'ltr'. Fonts: Rubik (300-900), Secular, Bangers, Fredoka, Deva (Devanagari) are loaded.
- SYNC IS WORD-EXACT: pops/landings happen ON the spoken word start. Word times: `words.js` (`WORDS` {w,t0,t1,ph}, seconds on the VOICE clock v). Start the motion a few frames early so the impact lands on `t0`; never late.
- The scene clock `s.t` is the VOICE clock v. During a HOLD (voice paused) v is frozen, so scene animations freeze. For idle life during holds use the output clock `A.T` (global, keeps running), e.g. drifting sparkles `A.T`.
- Captions (`main.js`) are drawn on top automatically: big gel word chips at y=1450 (`CL.capY`) with a small English strip below (~y 1540). Do NOT draw your own subtitles of the voice-over. Keep key content above y~1340 (or design around the band). Captions are hidden during holds and after v=27.6.
- Real logos (transparent PNGs): `CL.logoImg(name)`, `CL.logoCard(ctx,name,cx,cy,w,h,{rot,scale,alpha,fill})` (white gel card), `CL.logoGlow` (coloured rim for light logos on dark). Names: netflix, disney, appletv, prime, hbo, sport1..sport4, sport5 (+sport5live, sport5plus, sport5gold, sport5stars, sport5_4k), one, one2, kan11, keshet12, reshet13, ch14, ch9, i24, yes, yesbrand, hot, hotbrand. There is NO Charlton image: **Charlton = a designed glossy wordmark** "צ'רלטון" / "CHARLTON" (invent one, big, readable). Featured logos must be BIG (min ~420 px wide) and readable; check every logo on a rendered frame (a white logo on a white card is invisible).
- Each scene registers with `A.scene({name, start, end, draw(ctx, s)})`; `s.t` = voice-clock seconds, `s.lt` = seconds since scene start. A later-registered scene draws ON TOP of earlier ones. Every scene draws its own full-frame background (`CL.bg(ctx, t, {tint:[...]})`) and its own ENTRY TRANSITION: it starts ~0.25 s before its first word and covers the previous scene with a liquid wipe / splash flood / iris / bolt flash / card flip / squeeze (be creative, vary it; `CL.wave` is a starting point, and you may write your own). Scenes end ~0.4 s AFTER the next scene's first word (so the next has fully covered it).
- Do NOT edit shared files (engine.js, cl.js, main.js, words.js, lines.js, timeline.js, index.html). Put helpers you need inside your own file (IIFE). If you need a change in a shared file, say so in your final report. You MAY register per-hold camera focus lazily inside your `draw()`: `CL.HOLDFOC.cin = [x, y, zoom]` (focus point and zoom for the hold push-in; see main.js).
- Test: `cd /home/user/I/connecttv && node render.js --sheet A:B:N --prefix yourprefix --fast` (frame numbers = T*30 where T is OUTPUT time; before v=8.85 T=v; holds add: cin 8.85 to 10.25 output, tur v9.98 -> T 11.38 to 12.38, ind v11.03 -> T 13.43 to 14.53; see timeline.js `TLF.TofV(v)`); output `previews/yourprefix_A-B.jpg`; `node render.js --frames 90,120 --prefix x --fast` for single frames. Then LOOK at the image (Read it) and iterate until excellent. Use at most 1 render worker at a time (12 agents share the machine; the container has been OOM-killed before). Use `--fast` while iterating.
- Performance: cache static drawing with `CL.layer(key,w,h,fn)`; avoid ctx.filter and shadowBlur.
- Leave greppable cue comments for the sound team on every big moment, in VOICE-clock seconds: `// CUE 4.34 netflix-slam`, `// CUE 12.9 whoosh`.
- Final report: 5 lines max (what you built, times, anything for the director).
